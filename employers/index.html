Post a job How it works

What you get

  • Free posting. Post up to 5 jobs a month at no cost. If we add paid options later, the price will be shown before you pay, and nothing already free will become paid without notice.
  • Applicants in one place. See each applicant's profile, with a fit score and the reasons behind it, and sort by best fit or newest.
  • Private by default. Your phone number is not shown publicly. You chat with applicants in the app, and your own contact is shared with the worker you hire once they accept your offer.
  • Reviewed posts. Every post is checked before it goes live, which helps honest employers stand out from scams.
  • A Verified badge (optional). Send an ID photo and a selfie from your profile. If we approve them, a green Verified badge appears on all your posts. We delete the photos right after our decision.

Employer guidelines

Posts that break these rules are rejected or removed.

  • Post only real jobs you will hire for directly.
  • State honest pay that meets the legal minimum wage, and never ask applicants for a fee, deposit or deduction.
  • Describe the real duties, hours, days off and benefits, and don't change them in a misleading way after people apply.
  • Workers must be 18 or older, and requirements must not discriminate (for example by age).
  • Do not post for a recruitment agency or for work abroad unless the agency is licensed and shows its license number.
  • Follow Philippine labor law. For household workers, the Kasambahay Law (RA 10361) covers a written contract, a weekly rest day, 13th month pay, and SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG coverage.
  • Use applicants' details only for hiring, and keep them private.

Tips for a better job post

  • Use a clear title such as "Live-in yaya for two children" instead of "Hiring".
  • Say where and when: the barangay or area, the schedule, the start date and the days off.
  • Be specific about duties so applicants can decide if the job fits them.
  • Show the pay and benefits up front. Posts with clear pay get more relevant applicants.
  • Reply to applicants, even with a quick "not this time", and close the post when it is filled.
