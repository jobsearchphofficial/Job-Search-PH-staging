About Job Search PH

A free job board for Bacolod City and Negros Occidental.

What Job Search PH is

Job Search PH is an online job listing board based in Bacolod City. Employers post household, service and local jobs, jobseekers create a free profile and apply, and the two can talk to each other in the app. We currently list jobs in Negros Occidental only, and the site is in beta.

What we are not

We are a listing board, not an employer and not a recruitment agency. We don't recruit, screen, place or employ anyone, and we are not a party to any job agreement between an employer and a jobseeker. Employers hire directly. We never charge jobseekers.

How we try to keep listings trustworthy

  • Every job post is reviewed by our team before it goes live, and posts must state honest pay that meets the legal minimum.
  • Posts stay up for 30 days, and employers can mark a job filled so people stop applying.
  • Every job has a Report this post button, and we look into each report.
  • Employers and jobseekers can choose to Get Verified by sending an ID photo and a selfie. We check them, show a Verified badge if they pass, and delete the photos right after our decision.
  • Employer contact details are not shown publicly. Jobseekers message employers in the app, and the employer's own contact is shared once a job offer is accepted.
  • Chat messages that look like requests for money, fees or one-time codes are flagged with a warning.

None of this guarantees that any user is honest, so please read our safety tips.

Who it is for

Jobseekers looking for work such as kasambahay, yaya, caregiver, cook, driver, retail, food service and office jobs can browse open jobs. Employers, whether a family or a small business, can post a job for free.

Contact

Email jobsearchph.official@gmail.com, message us on Messenger, or visit our Facebook page. More ways to reach us are on the contact page.
