# Keyword strategy (hypotheses, not verified data)

**No search-volume, difficulty or ranking numbers exist here.** I had no SEO tool access. The only observation made: a web search for "jobs in Bacolod City hiring" (2026-10-28) showed Indeed, Talent.com, TTEC, PESO Bacolod / city government job-fair news and PNA articles. Large job boards own the generic "jobs in Bacolod" term. A new site is realistically more likely to win the **household-work and employer-intent** terms first. Check real volumes in Search Console (queries) and Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account) before investing in more pages.
