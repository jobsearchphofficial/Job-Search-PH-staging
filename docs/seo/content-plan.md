# Content plan

Rules: written by a person who knows the topic, accuracy-checked before publishing, no auto-publishing. Philippine legal claims (Kasambahay Law RA 10361, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, 13th month, minimum wage, DOLE/DMW) must be checked against DOLE or the law text on the day of publishing and dated. Include a "Last reviewed" date. Pages go in as static HTML in a `/guides/` folder; add them to `TRUST_PAGES`-style config and the sitemap when ready.
