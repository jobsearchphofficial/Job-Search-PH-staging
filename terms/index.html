01What Job Search PH is

Job Search PH ("JSPH", "we", "us") is an online job listing board that connects employers and jobseekers in Negros Occidental, Philippines. Employers post jobs, jobseekers apply with their profiles, and both sides talk directly.

We are not an agency or an employer. We don't recruit, screen, endorse, place, employ, supervise or manage anyone, and we're not a party to any job agreement made between users.

02Free to use

  • Searching and applying for jobs is free for jobseekers. We never charge jobseekers, in line with the Domestic Workers Act (RA 10361).
  • Posting a job is currently free for employers.
  • If we add optional paid features for employers later (for example, featured posts), the price will be shown clearly before you pay, and nothing already free will become paid without notice.
  • We don't charge placement fees.

03Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • Give true, current information. Use your real name and a mobile number you can be reached on.
  • Use one account per person. Don't create accounts to get around limits or restrictions.
  • Keep your password safe. You're responsible for what happens on your account.

04Job post rules

Employers agree that every post:

  • Is a real, current job they will hire for directly;
  • Pays at least the legal minimum wage and states the pay honestly;
  • Never asks applicants for any fee, deposit or deduction to apply or be hired;
  • Is only for workers aged 18 and older;
  • Has accurate duties, hours, days off and benefits, and isn't changed in a misleading way after people apply;
  • Doesn't include discriminatory requirements, such as age limits (Anti-Age Discrimination Act, RA 10911);
  • Isn't posted on behalf of a recruitment agency or for work abroad unless the agency is licensed by DOLE or DMW and the license number is included.

Posts are reviewed before going live and stay up for 30 days. We may reject, edit, close or remove any post.

05Employer responsibilities

  • Interview applicants and make your own hiring decisions;
  • Follow Philippine labor law, including the Kasambahay Law: minimum wage, weekly rest day, 13th month pay, a written contract, and SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG registration;
  • Provide safe, humane and respectful working conditions;
  • Use applicants' personal details only to consider them for your job, and don't share them.

JSPH isn't responsible for employer decisions, pay disputes, working conditions or private agreements.

06Jobseeker responsibilities

  • Give truthful information in your profile and applications;
  • Apply only to jobs you're genuinely interested in;
  • Communicate politely and honor interviews and job commitments, or tell the employer early if you can't;
  • Use "Leave this job" when a job ends so records stay accurate.

JSPH isn't responsible for false information given by jobseekers or for their behavior or work performance.

07Messages and conduct

  • Keep chats about the job. No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, or requests for money or sensitive personal data.
  • Chats with applicants who weren't selected close 48 hours after the decision.
  • To prevent spam, there are daily limits on applications, posts, messages and reports.
  • We may review chats when investigating a report.

We review job posts and look into reports, but we can't check every user or guarantee anything they say. We may warn, restrict, suspend or remove accounts and posts for fraud, misrepresentation, abuse, spam or breaking these terms. If you think we made a mistake, contact support to appeal.

  • Any job arrangement is made at your own risk;

JSPH isn't a party to any relationship or agreement between an employer and a jobseeker, and accepts no liability for any dispute, loss or harm between them, on or off the platform.
