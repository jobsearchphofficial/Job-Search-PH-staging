Help

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for jobseekers and employers. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Is Job Search PH free?
Yes. Creating a profile, searching and applying are free for jobseekers, always. Posting a job is free for employers.
Is Job Search PH a recruitment agency?
No. We're an online job listing board. Employers post their own jobs and hire directly. We don't recruit, screen, place or employ anyone, and we never charge placement fees.
How do I know a job is real?
Every post is reviewed before it goes live and must meet the legal minimum wage. Still, trust your instincts: if an employer asks for money, pushes you to leave the site quickly, or the details keep changing, stop and report the post.
Why can't I see the employer's phone number?
To protect both sides from spam and scams, you talk to employers through Messages here on Job Search PH. The employer's own contact is shared with you once you accept their job offer.
Someone asked me for money. What do I do?
Don't pay. Tap Report this post on the job, or contact support with the details. We'll review it and may remove the post or restrict the account.
How many jobs can I apply to?
Up to 10 a day. Employers can post up to 5 jobs a month. These limits stop spam and keep the site fair.
Why did my chat close?
If an employer chooses other applicants, the chat stays open for 48 hours so you can say goodbye or ask questions, then it closes. You can still read old messages.
How does hiring work?
When an employer picks you, they send a job offer. You'll get a notification and can Accept or Decline it from the job page, My applications, or the chat. You're only marked as hired once you accept. If you decline, the employer is told and the chat closes after 48 hours.
How do I leave a job I got here?
Go to My applications and tap Leave this job. Your employer confirms, and then you can apply to full-time jobs again. Employers can also start this from the applicant card.
How do I change or delete my profile?
Open the gear menu → My profile. You can edit everything there, or tap Delete my profile. To delete your whole account, email us.
I'm under 18. Can I use the site?
Not yet. Job Search PH is only for people 18 and older.
Questions? Email jobsearchph.official@gmail.com, message us on Messenger, or see our contact page.
