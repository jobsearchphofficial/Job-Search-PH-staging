Talk to us

Good to know

  • How do I pay? With GCash, Maya or any bank app, by scanning our InstaPay QR. We confirm it, usually within a few hours.
  • Is it free for jobseekers? Yes, always. Jobseekers never pay to apply, and employers must never charge them anything.
  • What if my post isn't approved? Fix it and we check it again. If we can't approve it, we refund your payment.
  • Do you take a fee when I hire? No. You pay only for the job post. Job Search PH is a job board: we don't recruit or place workers, and you choose who to hire.

Job Search PH is the local job board for Negros Occidental. We list jobs; we do not recruit, screen or place workers, and we never charge jobseekers.
