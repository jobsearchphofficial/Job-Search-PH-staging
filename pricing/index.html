How Pro works

  1. Write your post. About 5 minutes. Pick Pro at the end.
  2. Pay with GCash, Maya or your bank. Scan our InstaPay QR and type the reference number.
  3. Get applicants. We confirm, usually within a few hours, and your post goes to the top.

Questions

Why pay when the first post is free?

A free post stops at 5 applicants. Pro keeps taking applications and shows your post at the top, so more people see it and you have more to choose from.

Is there a fee when I hire someone?

No. You pay only for the job post. Job Search PH is a job board: we don't recruit or place workers, and you choose who to hire.

What if my post isn't approved?

We tell you what to fix and check it again. If we still can't approve it, we refund your payment.

Does Pro monthly renew by itself?

No. Nothing is charged automatically. When your 30 days end, you can pay again if you want to.

Is it free for jobseekers?

Yes, always. Jobseekers never pay to apply, and employers must never charge them anything.

Can I see my plan and receipts?

Yes. Sign in and open My plan & payments from the settings menu.
