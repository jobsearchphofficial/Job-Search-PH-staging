How Job Search PH works

Free for jobseekers. Free for employers to post.

For jobseekers

  1. Browse jobs. Search by job title or place, or browse all open jobs.
  2. Create a free profile. Add your contact details, the jobs you want, your experience and skills. You can apply to up to 10 jobs a day.
  3. Apply. The employer sees your profile. Applying is always free.
  4. Chat in the app. You and the employer message each other on Job Search PH, so neither side has to share a phone number first.
  5. Accept a job offer. When an employer picks you, they send a job offer. You are only marked as hired once you accept it, and the employer's own contact is shared with you then.

Want to hear about new jobs? Turn on Job alerts by email in your profile.

Create a free profile Browse jobs

For employers

  1. Create a free employer account and confirm your email.
  2. Post your job. Describe the duties, pay, hours and days off. You can post up to 5 jobs a month.
  3. We review it. New and edited posts are checked before they go live. A live post stays up for 30 days.
  4. Review applicants. Each applicant gets a fit score based on your post, and you can sort by best fit or newest.
  5. Chat, then send an offer. Talk to applicants in the app, meet them, and send a job offer to the person you choose.
  6. Close the post when the job is filled.

What Job Search PH does not do

We don't recruit, screen, place or employ workers, and we never charge placement fees. Hiring decisions, contracts and pay are between the employer and the worker. See our Terms of use.
