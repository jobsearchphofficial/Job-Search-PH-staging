Your data

Delete your account and data

You can delete your Job Search PH account and the information connected to it at any time. This includes anything we received when you signed in with Facebook or Google (your name, email and profile picture).

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Go to jobsearchph.com and sign in.
  2. Open My profile.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account, or go straight to jobsearchph.com/#/delete-account.
  4. Confirm. Your profile, job posts, applications, notifications and sign-in are deleted right away. Chats are closed and their messages are deleted after 30 days.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, email support@jobsearchph.com from the email on your account, or message our Facebook page. We delete your data within 30 days and reply when it's done.

Removing Job Search PH from Facebook

To stop Facebook sharing your information with us, open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Job Search PH and tap Remove. Then delete your account here as described above.

See also our Privacy notice.

Job Search PH is a job listing board. We do not recruit, screen or place workers, and we never charge jobseekers.
