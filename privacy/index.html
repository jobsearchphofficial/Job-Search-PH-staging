Privacy policy

How Job Search PH collects, uses and protects your personal data, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission.

01Data we collect

  • Account: your email and sign-in method (Google, Facebook, or email and password). If you use Facebook, we only receive your name, email and profile picture, and we never post to your Facebook.
  • Jobseeker profile: name, photo (optional), birthdate (we show employers your age, not your birthdate), gender, mobile number, city, Messenger, jobs wanted, experience, skills, work history, education and resume link.
  • Employer profile: name, photo (optional), household or business name, mobile number, city and Messenger.
  • Activity on the site: job posts, applications, chat messages, interview and hiring status, notifications, reports and support messages.
  • Get Verified (optional, for employers and jobseekers): a photo of your ID and a selfie holding it. They're stamped "Job Search PH · verification only" on your device before sending, and their location and camera details are removed. Only Job Search PH admins see them, only to confirm you're a real person. They're deleted as soon as we approve or reject, or after 14 days if we haven't. We keep only that you were verified, the type of ID and the date.
  • Anti-spam counters: how many applications, posts, messages and reports you sent today.
  • On your device only: your light or dark mode choice.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell your data.

02Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your posts, applications and messages;
  • To share your profile with employers you apply to, and employer contact details with the worker they hire;
  • To review job posts, prevent spam, fraud and abuse, and handle reports and appeals;
  • To reply to support requests and improve the site;
  • If you turn on job alerts, to email you new jobs that match the jobs and city on your profile. Emails are sent through our email provider, Resend. Unsubscribe with the link in any email or in My profile.

03Who can see it

  • Everyone: live job posts (title, employer or household name, area, pay and details). Employer contact details are not public.
  • Employers you apply to: your profile, photo, age, contact details, message, experience and resume.
  • The worker you hire: the employer contact you entered on the post, once they accept your job offer.
  • The person you're chatting with: your messages.
  • Job Search PH admins: all data, only to run and keep the site safe.
  • Service provider: the site runs on Google Firebase, which stores data for us under its own security and privacy terms.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, such as a lawful order from the authorities.

04How long we keep it

  • Your profile stays until you delete it.
  • Job posts expire after 30 days and stay in your account until removed.
  • Applications and chats stay so both sides can refer back to them. Chats with declined applicants close after 48 hours but remain readable.
  • Reports and safety records are kept as long as needed to protect users, then deleted.

05How we protect it

Data is stored on Google Firebase with access rules that let each person see only what they're allowed to. Admin access is limited to authorized Job Search PH staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

06Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you can:

  • Ask what data we have about you and get a copy;
  • Correct wrong information (most of it you can edit in My profile);
  • Delete your whole account yourself (My profile → Delete my account). Your profile, posts, applications and notifications are deleted right away; chat messages are deleted 30 days later (we keep them that long only to investigate scam reports);
  • Object to how we use your data, or withdraw consent;
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To make a request, email jobsearchph.official@gmail.com.

07Age limit

Job Search PH is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a minor has an account, tell us and we'll remove it.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here with a new date, and announce important changes on the site.

Questions? Email jobsearchph.official@gmail.com, message us on Messenger (facebook.com/thejobsearchph), or use Contact support. We're based in Bacolod City and serve Negros Occidental.

Job Search PH is a job listing board. We do not recruit, screen or place workers, and we never charge jobseekers.
